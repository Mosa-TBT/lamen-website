HOW IT WORKS

A clear path.
Every step.

Financing is a journey built on careful review, responsible decisions and a clear repayment plan. Follow the process from the first application to final settlement.

01 / 07GET STARTED

Application

The customer submits a financing request, explains the purpose of financing and provides the required identification, business information and supporting documents.

What happens: The request is registered and the initial documents are collected for review.
02 / 07DOCUMENT CHECK

FAD Review

The Financing and Administration Department (FAD) performs an initial review of the file, checks completeness and coordinates follow-up where information or documents are missing.

What happens: The application is organized for the next review stage, subject to LAMEN’s internal procedures.
03 / 07POLICY & RISK

Compliance Review

The relevant team reviews the file against applicable compliance requirements and internal policies, considering customer due diligence and risk-related questions where required.

What happens: Issues requiring clarification are identified before the file proceeds.
04 / 07APPROVAL AUTHORITY

Committee Review

The committee reviews the application and supporting assessment, then makes or recommends a decision in line with its delegated authority and LAMEN’s procedures.

What happens: The decision and any conditions are documented before disbursement preparation.
05 / 07DISBURSEMENT

Disbursement

After approval conditions are met and required agreements are completed, the financing is released according to the approved structure and documented transaction.

What happens: Disbursement is recorded and the repayment schedule is communicated to the customer.
06 / 07SCHEDULED PAYMENTS

Installment Repayment

The customer makes payments according to the agreed schedule. Each payment is recorded so that the paid amount, outstanding balance and upcoming due dates can be tracked.

What happens: The account is monitored through the repayment period until all required obligations are settled.
07 / 07FINANCING COMPLETE

Financing Closure

Once the final payment and any remaining obligations have been verified, the account is settled and the financing file is closed according to LAMEN’s procedures.

What happens: The completed financing is documented as settled, subject to final reconciliation and internal checks.
