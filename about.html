About LAMEN

Islamic microfinance, rooted in Afghanistan.

LAMEN Microfinance Institution, established in 2024, is a licensed microfinance institution committed to advancing financial inclusion across Afghanistan through responsible and Shariah-compliant financing.

Who we are

A financial partner for people building their livelihoods.

LAMEN provides Islamic, transparent and responsible financial services to individuals — both men and women — and to small businesses across Afghanistan.

Our financing is designed around real productive needs: a shop that needs inventory, a workshop that needs equipment, a farm that needs inputs, or a family improving its home. Each request is reviewed carefully and agreed on clear, Shariah-compliant terms.

We began financing operations in Kabul in July 2025 and now list branches in Kabul, Nangarhar, Kunar, Badakhshan and Baghlan, working close to the communities we serve.

    LAMEN is founded to widen access to responsible Islamic finance.

  2. March 2025 Authorized to operate

    MISFA reports LAMEN’s authorization from Da Afghanistan Bank to provide microfinance.

  3. July 2025 Operations launch

    Financing operations are inaugurated in Kabul, with the first client agreements signed.

  4. July 2025 Reaching Kunar

    A new branch brings Islamic microfinance to Kunar.

  5. December 2025 First MISFA facility

    An AFN 24 million Qard Hasan facility to expand Sharia-compliant financing.

  6. September 2026 Second facility

    MISFA announces a second AFN 56 million Qard Hasan facility.

Seven financing sectors

What LAMEN lists as eligible areas of activity on its official website.

  • Trade & small business

    Local shops and retail stores · Small and medium enterprises · Bakeries · Butcher shops and poultry supply · Food production and packaging · Handicrafts and home-based businesses

  • Agriculture & rural production

    Horticulture and agricultural products · Agriculture and livestock farming · Beekeeping and honey production · Fisheries and fish farming

  • Production & technical services

    Construction materials and work tools · Tailoring and garment production · Shoemaking and leather · Small workshops · Repair services · Solar energy systems · Commercial motorcycles and vehicles

  • Infrastructure & living standards

    Home and shop renovation · Drinking water systems and storage tanks · Water pumps · Cooling and heating equipment · Sanitation facilities

  • Technology & modern services

    Computers, laptops and office equipment · Smartphones · Barbering and grooming services

  • Social & public services

    Education, training and skills development · Healthcare and medical services · Essential ceremonial expenses · Emergency family financing

  • Special support programmes

    Support for women entrepreneurs · Support for young entrepreneurs · Family livelihood improvement

Source: LAMEN’s English homepage. Inclusion in a sector does not guarantee approval; every request is assessed individually.
